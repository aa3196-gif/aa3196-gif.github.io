LERI

LERI Rides is a student-to-student rideshare marketplace built specifically for the Rutgers University community to provide a safe, predictable $5 flat-rate alternative to campus buses. Unit Economics & Revenue Split: Fixed flat fare of $5.00 per ride anywhere on campus. $4.00 goes directly to the driver, while $1.00 is retained by the platform to cover payment gateway fees, hosting, and operational costs. Zero Surge Pricing Model: Replaces dynamic surge algorithms with a static pricing structure to keep rides affordable and predictable for college budgets. Low Overhead Dispatching: Utilizes a 24-hour advance scheduling marketplace rather than an expensive real-time algorithmic dispatch system, reducing idle driver cost and improving route density.
